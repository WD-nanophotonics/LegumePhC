# C3 PWE pilot evidence — 2026-09-05

Reproduction:

```powershell
.venv\Scripts\python.exe -m pytest -q
.venv\Scripts\python.exe run_c3_crosscheck.py --convergence
```

The run used Windows CPython 3.12, Legume 1.0.3, TE PWE, and the frozen
`configs/c3_benchmark.json`. Each geometry/cutoff solved the center and the
three M7 orbit points in one batch. Berry loops were piecewise-linear closed
loops with the declared steps `1/144`, `1/288`, and `0.001`; raw complex fields
were not compared.

The explicit qualification gates were frequency gap > `1e-3`, link magnitude
or rank-2 minimum singular value > `0.1`, and branch margin > `0.1` rad.
Frequency convergence additionally required the maximum change from the
previous cutoff ≤ `2e-3` and maximum orbit frequency residual ≤ `1e-3`.

At `gmax=5`, reciprocal-basis rank-2 covariance residuals were at most
`2.11e-2` for G15, `2.15e-2` for Circle, and `2.12e-2` for G16. The strict
geometry residuals were `2.12e-16` (G15), `0` (Circle), and `2.62e-2` (G16),
so G16 remains an approximate-C3 control and is not treated as a pass.

For the finest Berry step at `gmax=5`, rank-2 Wilson phases were 2.687 rad
(G15), 2.682 rad (Circle), and 2.687 rad (G16), with minimum link singular
values 0.634, 0.635, and 0.634 respectively. Rank-1 Berry is withheld by the
gap gate (the finest-cutoff gaps are below `1e-3`), even though the numerical
links and branch margins are reported.
